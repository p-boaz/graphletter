Tabletop Exercise Summary

Exercise Name: Phishing-to-Ransomware Response Drill
Date: March 4, 2026
Duration: 90 minutes
Facilitator: Security Program Manager

Purpose
Validate incident response roles, communication flow, and decision-making for a suspected ransomware event originating from a phishing email.

Participants
- Incident Commander (Security Lead)
- SOC Analyst
- IT Operations Lead
- Legal Representative
- HR Representative
- Communications Representative

Scenario Overview
At 9:15 AM, a finance employee reported a suspicious email and an encrypted file note on a shared drive. The SOC detected unusual lateral movement attempts from the employee workstation and elevated the event to a potential ransomware incident.

Timeline Highlights
- 9:20 AM: Incident declared and Incident Commander assigned.
- 9:25 AM: Affected workstation isolated from network.
- 9:35 AM: Shared drive access temporarily restricted.
- 9:50 AM: Internal stakeholder notification drafted and approved.
- 10:05 AM: Legal and HR reviewed external notification thresholds.
- 10:20 AM: Recovery and business continuity priorities confirmed.

Roles and Responsibilities Review
- Incident Commander coordinated response and approvals.
- SOC Analyst owned triage, indicators, and containment recommendations.
- IT Operations executed isolation and restoration planning.
- Legal advised on notification and recordkeeping requirements.
- HR supported employee communication and policy alignment.
- Communications prepared internal and external message templates.

Key Findings
- Escalation path was clear and followed correctly.
- Containment actions were timely.
- Notification drafting worked but took longer than target SLA.
- Asset owner list for one shared drive was outdated.

Action Items
- Update shared drive ownership registry by March 18, 2026 (IT Ops).
- Pre-approve two notification templates by March 22, 2026 (Legal + Comms).
- Run a follow-up 60-minute drill focused on executive notification by April 10, 2026 (Security).

Outcome
Exercise objectives were met. Team demonstrated adequate incident response role awareness with minor process gaps identified for remediation.
