# Graphletter golden-set labeling workbook

**How to use.** For each document: read it (full text in the linked `.txt`), then for every objective shown record YOUR call — `pass` / `partial` / `fail` / `na` — and for the control record YOUR maturity level `0`–`5`. The AI's answers are NOT shown (label blind). House rule: `na` = this artifact class could never evidence the objective; `fail` = it should and this document doesn't.

Only the first 5 objectives per control are listed (kept to ~90 min). Fill the `>>> YOUR CALL:` / `>>> YOUR LEVEL:` slots, then tell Claude to score you.
