ACME Corp — Third-Party Risk Management & Contracting Policy
Version 2.1 | Effective: January 2026 | Owner: CISO Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for managing cybersecurity risks associated with third-party service providers, vendors, and contractors who access, process, store, or transmit ACME Corp data or systems.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all third-party relationships involving access to ACME information systems, sensitive data, or critical infrastructure. Covers cloud service providers, SaaS vendors, outsourced IT, managed security providers, and contractors with system access.

3. THIRD-PARTY SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Contractual Security Obligations
All third-party contracts must include:
- Data protection and confidentiality clauses
- Security incident notification requirements (within 24 hours)
- Right-to-audit provisions
- Data handling, retention, and destruction requirements
- Compliance with applicable regulatory frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA as relevant)
- Business continuity and disaster recovery commitments
- Subcontractor flow-down requirements

3.2 Due Diligence
Prior to engagement, all third parties must:
- Complete a security risk questionnaire
- Provide evidence of security certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, or equivalent)
- Undergo risk classification (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
- Demonstrate data encryption capabilities (in transit and at rest)

3.3 Access Controls
- Third-party access limited to minimum necessary (least privilege)
- Dedicated accounts with MFA required for all system access
- VPN or zero-trust network access required for remote connections
- Access reviewed quarterly and revoked within 24 hours of contract termination

3.4 Data Protection
- Third parties must encrypt all ACME data using AES-256 or equivalent
- Data processing restricted to approved jurisdictions
- Data classification labels must be maintained by third parties
- Return or certified destruction of all data upon contract termination

4. MONITORING AND OVERSIGHT

4.1 Ongoing Monitoring
- Annual security assessments for Critical and High-risk vendors
- Continuous monitoring of vendor security posture via security ratings services
- Review of SOC 2 reports and penetration test results annually
- Tracking of vendor security incidents and remediation

4.2 Performance Metrics
- SLA compliance tracking for security-related KPIs
- Mean time to remediate identified vulnerabilities
- Incident response time compliance
- Patch management adherence

5. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
- Third parties must report security incidents within 24 hours
- Joint incident response procedures documented for critical vendors
- Forensic investigation cooperation required by contract
- Post-incident review within 30 days

6. TERMINATION AND OFFBOARDING
- Data return/destruction verification within 30 days
- System access revocation within 24 hours
- Certificate of data destruction required
- Post-termination audit rights for 12 months

7. COMPLIANCE
This policy aligns with NIST 800-53 SA-9, SA-12, ISO 27001 A.15, SOC 2 CC9.2, and SCF third-party management controls.

8. ENFORCEMENT
Violations may result in contract termination and legal action. Exceptions require CISO approval with documented risk acceptance.
