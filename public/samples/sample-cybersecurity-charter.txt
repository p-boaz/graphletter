CYBERSECURITY PROGRAM CHARTER
Version 2.1 — Effective January 15, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This charter establishes the organization's cybersecurity program and defines the governance structure, roles, responsibilities, and objectives for protecting information assets across all business units.

2. SCOPE
This program applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who access, process, store, or transmit organizational data and information systems. It covers all physical and virtual environments, including cloud infrastructure, on-premises systems, and remote work environments.

3. GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE
3.1 The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer and provides quarterly briefings to the Board of Directors.
3.2 A Cybersecurity Steering Committee, comprising senior leadership from IT, Legal, Risk, and Operations, meets monthly to review program status and approve strategic initiatives.
3.3 Domain-specific working groups (Identity & Access, Network Security, Application Security, Data Protection) report to the Steering Committee.

4. PROGRAM OBJECTIVES
4.1 Establish and maintain a risk-based approach to identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity threats.
4.2 Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards including NIST CSF, ISO 27001, GDPR, and PCI DSS.
4.3 Foster a culture of security awareness through regular training and communication programs.
4.4 Implement continuous monitoring and improvement of security controls.

5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Board of Directors: Provides oversight and approves cybersecurity strategy and risk appetite.
5.2 CISO: Leads program development, implementation, and maintenance. Manages the cybersecurity budget and team.
5.3 IT Department: Implements and operates technical security controls.
5.4 All Employees: Comply with security policies, report incidents, and complete required training.

6. RISK MANAGEMENT
The program employs a risk management framework aligned with NIST SP 800-37 to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity risks. Risk assessments are conducted annually and upon significant changes to the threat landscape or business operations.

7. INCIDENT RESPONSE
The organization maintains a documented incident response plan that is tested annually through tabletop exercises and simulation drills. All security incidents must be reported within 4 hours of detection.

8. METRICS AND REPORTING
Program effectiveness is measured through key performance indicators (KPIs) including mean time to detect (MTTD), mean time to respond (MTTR), vulnerability remediation rates, and security awareness training completion rates.

9. PROGRAM REVIEW
This charter is reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in the threat landscape, regulatory environment, or business operations. The next scheduled review is January 2027.
